How it works
  1. Write your lyrics, paste them, or press Oracle for a new song with verses and a chorus.
  2. Write a melody: choose a genre and press Sing it. Every press gives a new melody. The chorus always comes back the same.
  3. Your tempo decides the beat. Under the tempo you see which genres fit it.
  4. Load your song MIDI, for example the Chords file from a Studio, and the vocal follows its tempo, its key and its chords bar by bar.
  5. Sing my melody: load a MIDI melody. Every syllable gets a note. Extra notes stretch a syllable, extra syllables split a note. The timing stays exactly on your beat.
  6. Choose the voice you will sing with: the melody stays inside its range. NYL II sings English. Mo Xu sings English too when you set the language of the notes to English in Synthesizer V.
  7. Download the MusicXML, import it in Synthesizer V on bar 1, choose that voice. Vibrato, slides and tone you set in Synthesizer V itself.
