How it works: every tradition plays in its own language (kuchi-shoga, bols, Gun Go Pa, Dum Tek ka, conga strokes) and its own form: calls and answers, signals, breaks and cadences, as the players of that tradition do. The motor follows the parts of your song: quiet parts stay sparse, full parts open up. Made with deep respect for the drummers who carry these traditions; this is a sketchbook, a teacher is the real thing.
