How it works: every style is a set of traditional timelines (bell patterns, clave, maqsum, theka) and figures for each player. The motor combines them, lets a fourth bar answer the first three, adds and removes players every eight bars, slides a polyrhythm in halfway long sections and rolls into each new section. Fast tempos make slow styles play in half time, and the other way around.
